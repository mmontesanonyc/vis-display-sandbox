Asthma (ever) among NYC adults

Share of adults who have ever been told by a health professional that they had asthma, 2002–2020

Citywide, borough, race/ethnicity and sex lines are age-adjusted estimates. Age-group lines are unadjusted (crude) estimates. No survey data for 2005, 2015 and 2016, so lines connect across those gaps. Hover a point for the estimate and 95% confidence interval.

Goals of this display:
  • One option to show variability behind citywide trend - this could be an alternate display to the jitter display from 3iap
  • Only compare a single group to citywide, not group-to-group
  • Give a possibility to highlight group-to-whole differences if it's a meaningful difference.
